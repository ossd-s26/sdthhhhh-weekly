### Open Software Development Project

This week, I worked on fixing an issue related to audio focus behavior in the project ([Issue 356](https://github.com/tommyxchow/frosty/issues/356): Option to not take audio focus). The main goal was to give users more control over whether the app should take exclusive audio focus or allow other apps to continue playing audio.

To implement this, I first created a new branch dedicated to the fix. Then, I made two main changes. First, I added a new toggle in the central settings system to persist the user’s preference for sharing audio with other apps. This ensures that the app remembers the user’s choice across sessions.

<!--more-->

Second, I implemented an Android-specific UI switch in the “Android Video Settings” screen. This allows users to easily enable or disable the feature directly from the interface. Since this behavior is platform-specific, limiting the control to Android keeps the design clean and avoids unnecessary complexity on other platforms.

### Group Contribution

For group contribution, most team members, including myself, spent time analyzing the codebase and understanding how different components are structured and connected. This was important because the project is relatively large, and making changes requires a clear understanding of existing systems.

While working on our respective tasks, we were all actively exploring different parts of the code, identifying where changes should be made, and ensuring that our implementations fit into the overall architecture. This shared effort in codebase analysis helped the team move forward more efficiently and reduced the risk of conflicting changes.
